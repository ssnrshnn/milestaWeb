Everything in Milesta

🛤️

The Path

Every promotion, move, and adventure becomes a waypoint on a winding trail that's yours to scroll — on iPhone and iPad, in light or dark to match your phone. Your first 20 milestones are free; Premium lifts the limit.

✨

Recaps

Monthly, 6-month, and yearly summaries as beautiful Wrapped-style cards, posters, and 9:16 story images. Your latest monthly recap is free; the rest come with Premium.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no cloud, no ads. Your entries never leave your phone. Face ID lock, and anonymous usage statistics only if you opt in.

📊

Insights

Your milestone count and days on the path, free for everyone. Streaks, busiest months, categories, and places come with Premium.

🎬

Story replay

Watch your whole journey as a cinematic, story-style highlight reel — and relive anniversaries as full-screen memories. Both free for everyone; the replay appears once you have two milestones.

📍

Places & overview

Pin milestones to the places they happened, then zoom out to a bird's-eye view of every year at once. Both free — it's the places list inside Insights that comes with Premium.

🌍

15 languages

Fully localized, with right-to-left support and in-app language switching.

🖼️

Posters & widget

Share any milestone — or an anniversary — as a poster with your own photo, ready for your stories. Keep your newest one on the Home Screen, and add new ones from any app through the share sheet. All free for everyone.

💾

Yours to keep

One-tap portable backups and automatic monthly safety copies — all stored locally, and free for everyone. Export a full file, import it on a new iPhone, carry on.
