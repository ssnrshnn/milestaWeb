The path

It starts with one milestone.

A new job. A first home. A finish line. Give a moment a date, a place and a few photos, and it takes its place on a winding path you can scroll back through, newest at the top.

  1. 01

    Capture it

    Add a milestone in seconds, or save a note or a link from any app through the share sheet.

  2. 02

    Watch it grow

    Every milestone becomes a waypoint on your path, on iPhone and iPad, in light or dark.

  3. 03

    Relive it

    Recaps, posters and story replay bring the moments back, whenever you like.
